Goblin Helper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version. Goblin Helper runs on your computer. There is no account, no sign-up and no analytics, and nothing about you is sent to us in the background. Every feature that could read something about your machine is switched off when you install it, and stays off until you turn it on yourself. If you turn one off again, what it had collected is deleted. The only thing that ever reaches us is a suggestion or a bug report, and only when you write one and press send.

Who we are

Goblin Helper is made by Hallmelyn. You can reach us at goblinhelpercompany@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Goblin Helper desktop application for Windows. This page itself loads no fonts, no scripts and no images from anywhere else — there is nothing here that could track you either.

Nothing is collected until you switch it on

Goblin Helper can learn things about your computer so the goblin can be more useful: which programs you have, which games you play, how warm your hardware is running. None of that happens by default. Each source of information is a separate switch you will find in the app under Profile → Privacy, and every one of them ships turned off.

There is a seventh switch, separate from these, which decides something different: whether what the app already knows about your machine may be sent to an AI provider on the internet. It is also off by default. See The AI assistant.

Turning a switch off deletes what it collected. It does not merely stop collecting from that point on: the information that source had gathered is erased from your profile, and the change is written to disk immediately — not a few seconds later, so closing the app right after revoking cannot undo it.

Where your information lives

Everything Goblin Helper stores about you is a file on your own computer, under:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\GoblinHelper

That includes your profile, your settings, your reminders, your saved passwords, your game scores, the tips you have been shown and the history of your hardware readings. None of it is copied anywhere else. There is no account to create and nothing to log into, so there is no copy of your data waiting on a server somewhere: we run no service that stores it. The one server we do operate is the relay that carries a suggestion or a bug report to us when you send one, and it only ever carries what you wrote yourself — see Suggestions and bug reports.

Your passwords

The built-in password manager encrypts its vault on your machine before writing it to disk. The vault never leaves your computer and is never sent anywhere, in any circumstance. We cannot read it and we cannot recover it for you.

The AI assistant

The AI features are optional and are not configured out of the box. You can use them in one of two ways:

  • A local model, through Ollama. Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • A provider on the internet, with your own API key. The account and the key are yours; we are not in the middle and we never see your key, which is stored only on your machine.

When you use a provider on the internet, your question is sent to that provider and is subject to their privacy policy, not ours.

Context is a separate decision. The app can make its answers better by telling the model things it knows about your machine — your hardware, the programs you have, the game you are playing. That only happens if you switch on “share context with cloud AI”, which is off by default. With it off, your question still gets answered; it is just answered without knowing anything about you.

Suggestions and bug reports

If you use the in-app form to send us a suggestion, a bug report or a complaint, what you wrote and any screenshots you attached are sent to us. They travel through a relay we operate at goblin-relay.goblinhelpercompany.workers.dev, which passes the message on to us and keeps nothing else: no profile of you, no identifier, nothing about your machine. Nothing is attached automatically, and nothing is sent unless you press the button. We use it to fix and improve the app, and we do not use it for anything else.

Children

Goblin Helper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Since the app does not create accounts and does not collect personal information on any server, there is nothing for us to hold.

Your choices, and how to exercise them

Because everything lives on your computer, you are in control of it directly:

  • See what is on: Profile → Privacy shows every switch and its current state.
  • Withdraw a permission: turn the switch off. What that source collected is deleted at that moment.
  • Export your data: the app can write out what it holds about you.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app and delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\GoblinHelper. Nothing survives it, anywhere.

If you have a question about any of this, write to us and we will answer.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on its store page.
